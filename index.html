Why “New Wings”?

We began in 2020 as a handful of high-schoolers trying to do something — anything — during the pandemic. On 28 March 2021 we made it official. The name Naye Pankh stuck because that's exactly what we wanted to give people: a fresh start, and the lift to get there.

Our Story

Started by schoolkids.
Built into a movement.

NayePankh Foundation is a UP Government, 80G & 12A registered NGO, and one of the biggest student-led organisations in India — run almost entirely by people who are still in school or college.

2020

An idea, sparked by a crisis

As COVID-19 tore through the country, a small group of high-schoolers in Kanpur decided to use whatever they had — time, energy, a bit of pocket money — to help the people the pandemic was hitting hardest.

28 March 2021

Naye Pankh officially takes flight

What started as informal relief work became NayePankh Foundation — "giving wings" to the underprivileged. The team grew beyond one school, drawing students from across the city and state.

2021 – 2022

Beyond Kanpur

Operations expanded into Ghaziabad and other cities, and the focus widened from emergency food relief to four ongoing pillars of work: food, hygiene, clothing and education.

Today

12A & 80G certified, 200,000+ people reached

Every donation to Naye Pankh now carries a 50% income-tax exemption under Section 80G. More than two lakh people — and a fair number of street animals — have been helped along the way, with 30,000+ students trained through our internship programme.

What We Do

Four ways we give people their wings back.

Every programme answers one practical, everyday problem — hunger, dignity, warmth or opportunity — for communities who are usually last in line for all four.
